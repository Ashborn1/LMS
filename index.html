Library Rules & Guidelines

📌 Quick Rules

🎓 Student Role

What you CAN do:

  • Browse and search the book catalog.
  • Request to borrow available books (Max 5 total, 1 per title).
  • Join the waitlist for out-of-stock books.
  • Track your pending requests in "My Requests".
  • View your active loans and due dates.
  • Request loan extensions with a valid reason.
  • Submit support tickets for account issues.

What you CANNOT do:

  • Approve your own requests.
  • Add, edit, or archive books.
  • Manage other users' accounts.
  • Borrow more than 5 books or more than 1 copy of the same title.

📚 Bookkeeper Role

What you CAN do:

  • Add, edit, and archive books in the catalog.
  • Approve or reject student borrow requests.
  • Enforce Limits: Ensure students do not exceed 5 active loans or request more than 1 copy of the same title.
  • Set due dates when approving requests.
  • Process returned books from "Active Loans".
  • Review Extensions: Approve or reject loan extension requests based on the validity of the student's reason.
  • View the borrowing history.

What you CANNOT do:

  • Manage user accounts (Add/Edit/Archive).
  • Reply to Support Tickets (Only Admin can).
  • Borrow books (You are staff).

🛡️ Admin Role

What you CAN do:

  • Add, edit, and archive user accounts.
  • Unarchive previously archived users.
  • View and manage all Support Tickets.
  • Reply to tickets and close/reopen them.
  • View the full borrowing history.
  • Archive or restore history records.

What you CANNOT do:

  • Borrow books.
  • Approve or process borrow requests (Bookkeeper only).
  • Access the catalog view (Since you don't borrow).
